# Evaluation Protocol

## Presets

Evaluate P0, P1, P2, P3, P4a, and P4b with fixed dataset/codec/Cornerstone/network controls.

## Conditions

Record and compare:

- Cold start.
- RAM warm.
- OPFS warm.
- Cross-session authorised reuse.
- Restart behaviour.
- Permission denied and key-denied flows.
- Tampered/stale/interrupted entry cases.

## Metrics

- Median/p95 latency.
- Stalls and frame-identity correctness.
- Bytes transferred.
- Crypto/key/decode/render/write costs.
- Privacy/security outcomes and residual risks.

## Reporting constraints

Keep outcomes as proposed/planned unless verified. Do not disable security checks for timing. Use synthetic/de-identified data and controlled evidence handling.
