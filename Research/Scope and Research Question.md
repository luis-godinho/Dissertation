# Scope and Research Question

## Scope

Cybersecurity dissertation on privacy-preserving browser caching for medical-image viewing in the Dicoogle-next context.

In scope:

- Browser-side cache architecture trade-offs (P1-P4b).
- Security/privacy controls and limitations for browser execution/storage.
- Performance and user-perceived delay evaluation framing.

Out of scope for current claims:

- Autonomous DICOM-agent implementation.
- Cross-device/profile cache sharing.
- Claims of strict physical zero-footprint, universal clinical delay thresholds, or automatic legal compliance.

## Research questions

1. Can the proposed shared encrypted OPFS plus bounded RAM architecture provide acceptable responsiveness for defined medical-image tasks while preserving stated security/privacy boundaries?
2. How do P1-P4b differ in latency, cache reuse, privacy exposure, and operational limitations under controlled conditions?
